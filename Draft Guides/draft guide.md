# HAAG — Unit Researcher Guide

---

## What is a Unit?

- A unit is a group of HAAG projects organized around a similar research topic
- Your project will be assigned to a unit at the start of the semester
- Units meet bi-weekly as a group — this is separate from your individual project meetings
- The purpose is to share knowledge, see what other teams are working on, and collaborate across projects
- All researchers are expected to engage with their unit throughout the semester to receive full participation credit

---

## What You Are Required to Do

**1. Submit your weekly report to both channels**
Every Friday, submit your weekly report to your project channel and your unit channel. This keeps everyone in your unit informed of what each team is working on.

**2. Attend unit meetings**
Attend at least 3–4(still up in the air) unit meetings per semester. Attendance is tracked via an Excel sheet posted to the unit Slack channel after each meeting.(can have a llm/bot run attendance records)

**3. Participate in a working session**
Organize or join at least one self-organized working session with members from another team in your unit. A working session requires at least 2 teams and approximately 3 people.

**4. Report your working session**
After your working session, log it in the unit working session tracker and post an update to your unit channel. Include: date, teams involved, and attendees.

---

## After Each Unit Meeting

After every unit meeting, the following should be posted to the **#time-log** channel on HAAG Slack:
- Link to the meeting recording
- Attendance log
- AI-generated summary (created by copying the meeting transcript into an LLM)

For full instructions on how to do this, refer to the Unit Meeting Recording Instructions doc (Oliver Dobon).

## What Oliver's Recording Instructions Cover

Oliver Dobon (UI Unit, Fall 2025) created a step-by-step guide for post-meeting recaps. His doc covers:

- After each unit meeting, post **meeting video + attendance + AI summary** to the **#time-log** channel on HAAG Slack
- In the Teams chat, locate the recording and click **"View Recap"**
- Get the meeting link via **Share > Copy Link**
- Click **Transcript**, copy-paste the full transcript into an LLM to generate the AI summary
