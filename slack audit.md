## Cross-Channel Patterns

- **Scheduling reset every semester** — all five channels sent a when2meet at Spring 2026 start with no continuity from Fall
- **Faculty engagement drives activity** — the two most active channels both had Steve Mussmann; less active channels had advisors with constraints or limited presence
- **Unit manager turnover in multiple channels** — r-scripting, image-processing, and ui-unit all had turnover or uncertainty mid-semester
- **Members not knowing what the unit is for** — came up in at least two channels across two semesters
- **Zotero not functioning** — ui-unit had no library until Cody set one up mid-semester
- **Recording process inconsistent** — members asked about recordings across multiple channels with no standard answer
- **Internal HAAG scheduling conflicts** — ui-unit members cited another HAAG meeting as reason for missing unit meetings
- **Post-meeting materials inconsistent** — image-processing and methods shared consistently; 3d-vision and r-scripting had little to no post-meeting activity visible
