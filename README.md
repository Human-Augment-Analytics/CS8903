# HAAG Unit Meeting and Collaboration Initiative
Welcome to the Unit Meeting and Collaboration Initiative repository.
This repository contains the final procedures, onboarding resources, implementation documentation, and supporting materials developed during the Summer 2026 semester. The goal of this initiative was to standardize the HAAG unit meeting process by creating consistent onboarding resources for both faculty advisors and researchers while reducing the administrative burden placed on faculty.

# Problem and Scope

Prior to this initiative, unit meetings varied across HAAG research groups. Meeting expectations, onboarding procedures, and participant responsibilities were communicated differently depending on the research unit, resulting in inconsistent onboarding experiences and additional administrative work for faculty advisors.

The scope of this initiative was to develop standardized onboarding resources that clearly communicate unit meeting expectations for both faculty advisors and researchers. During the semester, the initiative evolved from revising the Unit Researcher Guide into developing separate onboarding presentations, scripts, and videos tailored to each audience.

---

# Intended Outcome

The intended outcome of this initiative was to create a repeatable onboarding process that future HAAG units can use when introducing new researchers and faculty advisors. By providing audience-specific onboarding materials, the initiative aims to improve consistency across research groups while allowing faculty to focus on mentorship rather than administrative responsibilities.

---

# Solutions and Work Completed
During the semester, the working group completed the following:

- Developed a Faculty Onboarding Procedure.
- Developed a Researcher Onboarding Procedure.
- Created faculty and researcher onboarding PowerPoint presentations.
- Wrote onboarding scripts for both audiences.
- Recorded onboarding videos introducing the standardized unit meeting process.
- Revised onboarding materials based on stakeholder and project manager feedback.
- Prepared documentation supporting future implementation and dissemination.

## Presentation Feedback and Reflection

During the final presentation, a key discussion focused on how the onboarding resources should be disseminated throughout HAAG. Our proposed approach was to distribute the Unit Researcher Guide together with the faculty and researcher onboarding videos using HAAG's peer survey platform, allowing approximately 25–50 participants to review the materials and provide structured feedback before broader adoption.

Due to the compressed summer semester and changes in the initiative's scope, the team did not have sufficient time to disseminate the Unit Researcher Guide or the onboarding materials, nor to formally evaluate their effectiveness. Future HAAG teams should distribute these resources to researchers and faculty, collect structured feedback, evaluate the onboarding experience, and refine both the guide and onboarding materials before integrating them into the standard HAAG onboarding process.
