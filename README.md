# HAAG Unit Meeting and Collaboration Initiative

Welcome to the **Unit Meeting and Collaboration Initiative** repository.

This repository contains the final procedures, onboarding resources, implementation documentation, and supporting materials developed during the Summer 2026 semester. The purpose of this initiative was to standardize the HAAG unit meeting process by creating consistent onboarding resources for both faculty advisors and researchers while allowing faculty advisors to focus on mentorship rather than administrative responsibilities.

This repository is intended to preserve the team's work so future HAAG members can implement, evaluate, and continue improving the onboarding process.

# Problem and Scope

Before this initiative, unit meetings were conducted differently across HAAG research groups. Meeting expectations, onboarding procedures, and participant responsibilities varied between units, resulting in inconsistent onboarding experiences for both researchers and faculty advisors. Faculty often needed to repeatedly explain meeting logistics and researcher expectations, increasing the administrative effort required to mentor new researchers.

The objective of this initiative was to create standardized onboarding resources that clearly communicate the unit meeting process while providing separate onboarding experiences for faculty advisors and researchers. During the semester, the initiative evolved from revising the Unit Researcher Guide into developing dedicated onboarding presentations, scripts, and videos tailored to each audience.

---

# Intended Outcome

The intended outcome of this initiative was to develop a repeatable onboarding process that future HAAG units can use when introducing new researchers and faculty advisors. By creating role-specific onboarding materials, the initiative aims to improve consistency across research groups while ensuring that faculty advisors can focus on mentorship and researchers clearly understand their responsibilities within the unit meeting system.

---

# Solutions and Work Completed

The following deliverables were completed during the semester:

- Developed a standardized Faculty Onboarding Procedure describing advisor responsibilities, researcher accountability, and the faculty role within the unit meeting process.
- Developed a standardized Researcher Onboarding Procedure describing researcher roles, update templates, unit meetings, post-meeting responsibilities, working sessions, and grading expectations.
- Created separate onboarding PowerPoint presentations for faculty advisors and researchers.
- Wrote onboarding scripts for both onboarding videos.
- Recorded faculty and researcher onboarding videos introducing the standardized unit meeting process.
- Revised onboarding materials throughout the semester based on project manager, instructional staff, and presentation feedback.
- Prepared documentation supporting future implementation and dissemination throughout HAAG.

# Presentation Feedback and Reflection

During the final presentation, discussion focused on how the onboarding materials should be disseminated throughout HAAG once the initiative is adopted. A proposed next step was to distribute the Unit Researcher Guide together with the faculty and researcher onboarding videos through HAAG's peer survey platform, allowing approximately 25–50 participants to review the materials and provide structured feedback before broader implementation.

Due to the compressed summer semester and changes in the initiative's scope, the team did not have sufficient time to disseminate the Unit Researcher Guide or the onboarding materials, nor to formally evaluate their effectiveness. Future HAAG teams should distribute these resources to researchers and faculty, collect structured feedback, evaluate the onboarding experience, and refine both the guide and onboarding materials before incorporating them into the standard HAAG onboarding process.
