## Feedback on Draft Researcher Guide

**Rewrite around the project rep model:**
- Every project sends one representative to the unit meeting — not everyone attends
- The rep is responsible for collecting updates from team members who are not attending, consolidating into something presentable, posting an agenda before the meeting, and generating a report after
- Non-attending team members also have requirements — idea: they send a slide to the rep explaining what they are working on, and the rep needs to understand it well enough to present it
- Beginning, middle, and end structure — agenda before, slides during, report after — all on the rep, not the unit manager

**On specificity:**
- No level of granularity is too specific — if it is not written down, it will not happen
- The doc needs to account for what a brand new researcher knows, which is nothing
- Even Oliver's recording instructions need more detail — step by step, button by button
- Provide a slide template for what the meeting slide should look like
- Mention Otter AI as optional but the researcher's own problem to figure out

**On meeting format:**
- Two options: standup format or one presenter per week
- Give a clear default — Bri leans toward standup format with slides from the rep
- Could offer multiple formats but would then need a way to track which format is being used and how effective each is

**On enforcement:**
- Cannot just write what should happen — must write how it is enforced
- Bot idea: automated weekly Slack reminders asking for agenda, post-meeting tasks, and session reports, with a note that responses are monitored for grading
- Bot responses feed into an Excel sheet visible to staff — flag anyone who has not responded multiple times in a row
- Dima has already built something like this for Qualtrics surveys — reach out to her
- Sean has also done Slack automation work — reach out to him
- Document whatever bot is built so HAAG knows how it works going forward
- Remove all references to extra credit — does not scale at HAAG's size and is too hard to track
- Grading enforcement at baseline: bot reminds researchers their participation is being monitored for their grade

**On faculty:**
- Faculty's only job is to show up and give advice — all organization falls on researchers
- Mussmann's units work because he is putting in extra effort, not because researchers are more organized — this is the exception, not the model to replicate
- The goal is to make it easy enough for any faculty to walk in and just be a faculty advisor
