## Bri's Feedback — Section by Section

### Role of Faculty
- Add a caveat: not all units will have a faculty advisor
- If a new unit is started, it may operate without a faculty advisor at first and get advertised to faculty later — could be a mid-semester join
- It is also possible a **computational advisor** leads a unit instead of faculty, if no faculty is available but a comp advisor wants to lead
- Units should still meet and discuss even without a faculty or comp advisor present — this should not block meetings from happening

### Project Representative / Rep Rotation
- Do not phrase this as a "rotation" — it currently reads as if the whole team rotates attendance at different meetings, which would create a scheduling nightmare since the meeting time would need to accommodate everyone's rotating availability
- Rewrite as: **the team designates one person** to be the presenter. Everyone not attending sends information to be presented to that person. That person collects information from faculty/advisors during the meeting and sends it back to the team. That person needs to be a good facilitator
- **Multiple representatives are allowed** — the requirement is only one person minimum, but if an entire team wants to attend, that is fine and actually preferred. State this explicitly so people do not think they are barred from attending if they want to
- **If the designated rep cannot attend:** the responsibility falls on the rep to find a substitute from the team. The team should make a best effort to send someone in their place, since the rep is doing extra organizational work the rest of the team does not have to do

### Recording Requirement (New)
- Unit meetings should be recorded, and it should be a requirement that everyone watches the recording regardless of whether they attended — this way information only needs to flow one direction instead of the rep having to relay everything secondhand, which risks losing information

### Slide Content
- One slide per person may not give the rep enough context to actually understand and represent someone else's work
- Non-attending members should add **comments or notes on the slide** — essentially what they want the rep to say when presenting it
- **New scenario to plan for:** if faculty is confused by a slide and the rep cannot answer because it is not their own work, the responsibility falls on the **original slide owner** to clarify — via async follow-up in the unit Slack channel
- **New requirement:** once the meeting recording is posted, the researcher whose work was presented must respond to any questions raised about their work in that thread

### Bot — Missing Slide Tracking (New Idea)
- The rep should be able to flag "missing slide from [name]" during or after the meeting, and that gets logged by the bot as a missed submission for that person — this is a cleaner way to track non-submission than relying solely on the bot's own reminder responses

### Meeting Time Management
- Flag: in past semesters some projects have taken up too much meeting time and gotten more attention than others, at the expense of other projects
- No clean fix identified, but consider having someone in charge of timekeeping during the meeting, or think about an automated way to manage this

### General
- Overall very positive — Bri said she really likes how the guide turned out
- With a few tweaks, the guide is in a good place to start piloting and testing effectiveness
