## Discussion: Extension vs. Standalone Paper

**Initial framing:** I explained that my work closely relates to FAIR-CS, specifically to a gap identified in its future work section around the difficulty of managing a distributed network of researchers and sustaining cross-team collaboration at scale.

**Charlie's initial reaction:** After reviewing the first page or two of my researcher guide live on the call, he initially leaned toward framing this as an extension of FAIR-CS, since it builds directly on HAAG's structure as defined in that paper (researchers, faculty affiliates, computational advisors, role tasks).

**The key question Charlie asked:** Does this work apply to non-HAAG-like organizations, or is it conceptually tied to HAAG specifically?

**My answer:** Yes, it could apply broadly. If the researcher-specific terminology is removed, any team-based organization could reuse the framework.

**Ahsan's input (joined partway through):** He said the documentation is thorough enough that it has value outside of academia entirely, comparable to something an organization might pay a consultant to produce if standing up a new team or ad hoc group. He supported continuing and publishing it broadly rather than narrowing the scope.

**Charlie's revised position:** Framing the paper as a strict extension of FAIR-CS would limit the audience and require reconciling with FAIR-CS's terminology, which was itself fairly terminology-heavy. Since Bri has already directed me to strip HAAG-specific language so other organizations can use the paper, trying to closely tie this to FAIR-CS's specific framing would work against that goal.

---

## Final Recommendation

**Do not frame the paper as a direct extension of FAIR-CS.** Instead:

- Mention FAIR-CS as a building block or source of inspiration
- Cite it appropriately so it doesn't look overlooked if a reader is already familiar with it
- Do not structure the entire paper around FAIR-CS's terminology or framework
- Keep the paper generalized and standalone, applicable beyond HAAG and beyond academic research organizations specifically

Charlie's suggested framing: **"inspired by FAIR-CS, but not an exact continuation of it."**

He noted this is his opinion and recommended I confirm the decision with Bri directly, since she may not respond immediately.
