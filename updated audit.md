## Project Channel Audit

A brief scan of active project channels was conducted to identify any collaboration patterns worth incorporating into the researcher guide. Most channels consist only of weekly report PDF submissions with no additional activity. The channels below were the exception.

- **vectordb-retrieval** — Pranav built a self-initiated Project Tracker tab in Slack with tasks, status, and due dates, updated after every biweekly meeting
- **porto-3d-fossils** — Advisor posted a structured follow-up after the first meeting with homework, links, weekly report instructions, and an example submission
- **LLM interpretability** — Kaiyuan Deng set up an automated bot that posts every Friday asking members to submit their weekly report and confirm with an emoji
- **animal-detection** — Mohammed posted a meeting summary with attendees, individual updates, blockers, and action items — rotates week to week

**Key finding:** The channels with meaningful activity had one thing in common — someone took individual initiative to add structure that was not required. Nothing was mandated. This supports the case for the researcher guide explicitly requiring these practices rather than leaving them to individual initiative.
