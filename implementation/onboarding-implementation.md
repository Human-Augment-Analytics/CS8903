# Onboarding Implementation

## Procedure Implemented

This implementation document relates to the following procedures:

- Faculty Onboarding Procedure
- Researcher Onboarding Procedure

The goal of the implementation phase was to prepare standardized onboarding resources that could be introduced to future HAAG faculty advisors and researchers.

---

# Context and Participants

The Unit Meeting and Collaboration Initiative was completed during the Summer 2026 semester as part of the Human Augmented Analytics Group (HAAG).

Participants included:

- Arjun Sivanesan
- Minkyung Oh

Project oversight and feedback were provided by:

- Kevin Lemus-Medrano (Project Manager)

The onboarding materials were developed for future faculty advisors and researchers participating in HAAG unit meetings.

---

# Implementation Approach

Rather than immediately deploying the onboarding process, the team focused on creating a complete set of standardized onboarding resources.

The implementation work included:

- Developing faculty onboarding procedures.
- Developing researcher onboarding procedures.
- Creating separate onboarding presentations for faculty and researchers.
- Writing onboarding scripts.
- Recording onboarding videos.
- Revising materials based on project manager and instructional feedback.
- Preparing documentation for future dissemination and implementation.

---

# Outcomes and Evidence

The onboarding materials were successfully completed by the end of the semester.

Deliverables included:

- Faculty onboarding procedure.
- Researcher onboarding procedure.
- Faculty onboarding presentation.
- Researcher onboarding presentation.
- Faculty onboarding video.
- Researcher onboarding video.
- Updated repository documentation.

Although implementation was not completed during the semester, the onboarding resources are prepared for future dissemination and evaluation.

---

# Effectiveness

The initiative successfully produced a standardized set of onboarding resources for both faculty advisors and researchers.

Separating the onboarding experience by audience provides role-specific guidance while creating a consistent meeting process across HAAG research units.

Although the onboarding materials were not formally evaluated, the completed resources provide a strong foundation for future implementation.

---

# Challenges and Limitations

Several factors limited implementation during the Summer 2026 semester.

- The initiative scope changed multiple times throughout the semester.
- Additional onboarding videos for both researchers and faculty were added after the initial project scope.
- The compressed summer semester limited the available implementation timeline.
- There was insufficient time to disseminate the Unit Researcher Guide or onboarding videos.
- Formal evaluation and collection of participant feedback could not be completed before the end of the semester.

---

# Next Steps

Future HAAG teams should:

1. Disseminate the Unit Researcher Guide.
2. Distribute the faculty and researcher onboarding videos.
3. Collect structured feedback from approximately 25–50 researchers and faculty using HAAG's peer survey platform.
4. Evaluate the effectiveness of the onboarding materials.
5. Revise the onboarding resources based on collected feedback.
6. Integrate the finalized onboarding process into future HAAG units.
