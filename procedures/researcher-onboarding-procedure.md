# Researcher Onboarding Procedure

## Purpose

The Researcher Onboarding Procedure provides a standardized introduction to HAAG's Unit Meeting and Collaboration process for researchers. Its purpose is to establish clear expectations for participation, define researcher responsibilities, and ensure consistency across all HAAG research units.

---

# Scope and Use

This procedure should be completed by all researchers when joining a HAAG unit.

It explains the structure of unit meetings, researcher roles and responsibilities, required meeting deliverables, working sessions, and grading expectations.

This procedure complements the Unit Researcher Guide and should be completed before participating in unit meetings.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Project Representatives

Project representatives are responsible for:

- Attending every scheduled unit meeting.
- Collecting updates from all members of their project.
- Preparing the meeting presentation and agenda.
- Presenting project progress during the meeting.
- Finding an alternate representative if they cannot attend.
- Posting the meeting recap after the meeting.

## Non-Participating Members

Researchers who are not attending a particular meeting are responsible for:

- Completing the required update template before the meeting.
- Providing notes or discussion points for the project representative.
- Assisting with project updates as needed.

## All Researchers

All researchers are expected to:

- Participate in required working sessions.
- Respond to follow-up questions when necessary.
- Contribute to project progress throughout the semester.

---

# Tools and Prerequisites

Before participating in a unit meeting, researchers should review:

- Researcher Onboarding Presentation
- Researcher Onboarding Video
- Unit Researcher Guide
- Required Update Template

---

# Step-by-Step Process

1. Review the Researcher Onboarding Presentation.
2. Watch the Researcher Onboarding Video.
3. Review the Unit Researcher Guide.
4. Complete the required update template before each unit meeting.
5. Representatives prepare the meeting presentation and agenda.
6. Participate in the scheduled unit meeting.
7. Share progress, receive feedback, and discuss project challenges.
8. Representatives post the meeting recap, AI-generated summary, recording link, attendance list, and action items within 24 hours.
9. Participate in at least one working session with another project during the semester.
10. Complete all required reports and meeting documentation.

---

# Outputs and Success Criteria

Researchers who complete this onboarding procedure should:

- Understand the purpose of the unit meeting system.
- Understand representative and non-participating member responsibilities.
- Know how to complete update templates.
- Know how unit meetings and working sessions operate.
- Understand grading expectations and required documentation.
- Be prepared to actively participate in their assigned unit.

---

# Lessons Learned and Future Improvements

Throughout the semester, this onboarding procedure was refined through project manager and instructional feedback. Due to the compressed summer semester and changes in initiative scope, the onboarding procedure was completed but not formally disseminated or evaluated.

Future HAAG teams should distribute the onboarding materials to incoming researchers, collect structured feedback, evaluate the onboarding experience, and continue refining the procedure based on future implementation.
